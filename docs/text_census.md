## English estimate and Lord of the Rings comparison

The estimate assumes about 2.2–3.0 content characters per English word. The script is colloquial and heavy on kana, so the ratio is toward the high end. That gives **about 150k–200k English words, central estimate ~180k**.

Lord of the Rings (English): Fellowship 187,790 + Two Towers 156,198 + Return of the King 137,115 = **~481k words**.

| | Words | vs LotR trilogy |
|---|---:|---:|
| Super Tokusatsu Taisen 2001 (est.) | ~150k–200k | **~31–42%** (about 0.4×) |
| The Fellowship of the Ring | 187,790 | 39% |

The game script is roughly **the length of The Fellowship of the Ring**, or a bit under half the trilogy.

## Not counted

- Text drawn inside images: attack-name banners in WAZA1-3.DAT (CM-compressed), title and menu graphics, and the stage-title cards.
- The Shift-JIS memory-card save title in the executable.
